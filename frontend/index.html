Villages waiting to welcome you

Every village on ExploreMyVillage is home to real families who open their doors, fields and kitchens to travellers like you.

Aambadi

Maharashtra

A palm-fringed Konkan village known for Alphonso mango orchards, red-tiled homes and quiet fishing coves.

Tirthan Valley

Himachal Pradesh

Wooden Kathkuni houses tucked beside a glacial river, surrounded by apple orchards and pine forest trails.

Khimsar

Rajasthan

Sand dunes, camel herders and centuries-old block-printing workshops on the edge of the Thar Desert.

Kumarakom

Kerala

Coconut-lined backwaters, houseboat families and generations-old coir-weaving traditions.
